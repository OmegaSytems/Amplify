QUESTIONS

Before we get started.

Can we use one service without purchasing all four?

Yes. Your plan can be scoped around Paid, Organic, Leads Only or Leads + Closing. Services can be combined when a shared strategy makes sense.

Is out-of-home advertising available?

OOH can be assessed as part of a paid-media plan. Placement options and costs are subject to real supplier availability, location permissions and a suitable business case. No specific inventory or price is guaranteed here.

How do approvals and reporting work?

Each engagement should define approval owners, reporting cadence and the results being measured. The new client portal is under development; it is not yet a live production service.

Is there a fixed package or price?

Scope depends on the service mix, advertising budget, volume, channels, production and follow-up requirements. A proposal should separate service fees from media and other external costs.
